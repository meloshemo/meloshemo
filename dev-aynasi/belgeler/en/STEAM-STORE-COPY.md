# Steam Store Copy — Dev Aynası (Hall of Giants)

## Name
```
Dev Aynası: Hall of Giants
```

## Short description (max 300 characters)
```
A dark hall of a thousand identical mirrors. Every one shows you as you are;
only one makes you a giant. Find it, walk into it. Six rooms, six rules — and
at the end you still have to find your way back to yourself.
```

## About This Game
```
[h2]See yourself in the giant mirror[/h2]
"Kendini dev aynasında görmek" is a Turkish idiom: to see yourself in the giant
mirror — to think yourself far greater than you are. Dev Aynası turns that
idiom into a game you play.

With a lantern in hand you walk a funfair hall whose every wall is a mirror.
Step close and your reflection appears behind the glass — a real one, drawn by
reflecting you across the plane of the mirror. Every pane shows you as you are.
Only one shows you in gold, a giant too large for its frame.

Find it, walk into it, and the next room opens.

[h2]Sixteen rooms[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]I · Hall of Mirrors[/b] — Find the giant mirror and walk into it
[*][b]II · Inside the Mirror[/b] — Tell the real giant from the crooked ones
[*][b]III · Inverted Hall[/b] — Controls are mirrored: press left, go right
[*][b]IV · Shifting Mirrors[/b] — The walls will not hold still
[*][b]V · The Echo[/b] — A twin repeats your every move, reversed
[*][b]VI · Room of Pride[/b] — The brightest room of pride
[*][b]VII–XII[/b] — Paris, Venice, Tokyo, New York, Cairo, Istanbul
[*][b]XIII–XV[/b] — London fog, Dubai glass tower, Rio carnival
[*][b]XVI · The Fleeing Mirror[/b] — The giant mirror will not stay; approach and it flees
[/list]

[h2]Alone or head to head[/h2]
[list]
[*]Two players on one keyboard, split screen, racing through the same hall
[*]Hall codes: anyone entering the same code plays the exact same hall
[*]Continue where you left off: room, hall and time are kept even if you close the game
[*]Progress code: move your progress from phone to desktop with a single code
[*]Endless hall: a never-ending run of randomly combined rules
[*]Online race in development: the more players, the bigger the hall — up to 26,000 mirrors
[/list]

[h2]Details[/h2]
[list]
[*]Every mirror draws a true reflection
[*]Silent by design: no sound or music, attention stays on the search
[*]Turkish and English; light, no install, 60 FPS on modest hardware
[/list]
```

## Controls
```
W A S D / arrow keys — walk
Shift — run
H — hunch (points toward the giant mirror for a moment)
R — new hall
Duel: player 2 uses arrow keys + M
```
