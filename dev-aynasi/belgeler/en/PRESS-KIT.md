## One line

In a dark hall of a thousand identical mirrors, you search for the one that
makes you a giant.

## Short description (~300 characters)

A dark hall of mirrors. A thousand panes, every one the same, every one showing
you exactly as you are. Only one makes you a giant. Find it, walk into it — and
through six rooms, find your way back to yourself.

## Long description

"Kendini dev aynasında görmek" is a Turkish idiom: to see yourself in the giant
mirror, meaning to think yourself far greater than you are. Dev Aynası turns
that idiom into a game you play.

With a lantern in hand you walk a funfair hall whose every wall is a mirror.
Step close and your reflection appears behind the glass — a real one, drawn by
reflecting you across the plane of the mirror. Every pane shows you as you are.
One shows you in gold, a giant too large for its frame. Find it, walk into it,
and the next room opens.

Twenty rooms, twenty rules: crooked mirrors that mislead you, a room where the
controls are mirrored, a room where the walls will not hold still, a room where
an echo repeats your every move in reverse, and finally the Room of Pride —
where finding the mirror is not enough, because you still have to find your way
back.

## Key features

- **Real reflections.** Every mirror reflects you across its own plane.
- **Thousands of mirrors.** Over 1,600 panes in the first room alone; online
  rooms scale up to 26,000.
- **Sixteen rooms.** Crooked mirrors, inverted controls, shifting walls,
  a mimicking echo, a dying lantern.
- **Split-screen duel.** Two players, one keyboard, the same hall.
- **Endless hall.** A never-ending run of randomly combined rules, with a best-run record.
- **Continue where you left off.** Room, hall and time survive closing the game.
- **Progress code.** Move your progress from phone to desktop with one code.
- **Hall codes.** Anyone entering the same code plays the exact same hall.
- **Silent by design.** The game has no sound or music; attention stays on the search.
- **No install.** Runs in a browser; a single-file offline build is included.

## Rooms

| Room | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| I · Hall of Mirrors | Find the giant mirror and walk into it |
| II · Inside the Mirror | Tell the real giant from the crooked ones |
| III · Inverted Hall | Controls are mirrored: press left, go right |
| IV · Shifting Mirrors | Walls change places every 11 seconds |
| V · The Echo | A twin repeats your every move, reversed |
| VI · Room of Pride | The brightest room of pride, 24 crooked giants |
| VII–XII | Paris, Venice, Tokyo, New York, Cairo, Istanbul — each city its own rule |
| XIII–XV | London (fog), Dubai (glass tower), Rio (carnival) |
| XVI · The Fleeing Mirror | The giant mirror moves every 20 seconds and flees when you approach |
| XVII · Kyoto | Still Water: walking scatters the reflection, stillness gathers it |
| XVIII · Reykjavík | Hall of Ice: friction drops to a tenth — you slide |
| XIX · Marrakesh | The Dying Lantern: only first-seen glass feeds the flame |
| XX · Infinity | The Lying Mirrors: some panes show a giant but break symmetry |
